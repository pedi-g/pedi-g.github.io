01About

Understanding ecological communities.

I am a researcher with a background in marine benthic ecology. My work combines field observations, species identification, functional traits, and ecological data analysis.

I hold an MSc in Animal Biology–Biosystematics from the University of Tehran, where I studied benthic communities in natural and planted mangroves in the Gulf of Oman under the supervision of Dr Reza Naderloo. I also hold a BSc in Mechanical Engineering from Shahid Beheshti University.

My broader interests include macroecology, biogeography, and eco-evolutionary dynamics. I am particularly interested in mechanistic and predictive approaches that help explain how ecological communities assemble, persist, and respond to environmental change.

02Research

Species, traits & environmental change.

My research experience centres on benthic invertebrate communities in coastal ecosystems, examining variation in species composition and functional traits across habitats, environmental conditions, and seasons.

Featured research · Gulf of Oman

Natural and planted mangroves

My MSc research compared macrobenthic communities at natural and planted mangrove sites using community sampling, functional traits, and water and sediment measurements.

The sites differed in species and trait composition alongside sediment characteristics. With one natural and one planted site, differences cannot be attributed to planting history alone.
